How selling an NFT instantly works

Accept a bid that is already live instead of listing and hoping a buyer appears. Here is what sits behind the offer.

What selling instantly means

It means accepting the best current bid on a token now, trading some price for guaranteed liquidity instead of waiting on an open listing. The bid you accept settles on-chain in one transaction that transfers the token and releases payment at the same moment, with no escrow step and no risk the other side walks away.

Collection bids and trait bids

A collection bid is an open offer on any token in a collection, so a holder can match the top offer and exit without needing a buyer for that exact piece. A trait bid narrows the offer to tokens with a specific attribute and usually pays more. Checking for a matching trait bid before taking the generic collection offer is one of the easiest ways to capture a better price.

NFT-AMM pools

Automated pools hold a collection plus the matching currency and price each trade along a curve rather than through individual bids. Selling into a pool is close to instant because the contract itself is the counterparty, though thin pools can sit further below floor and move sharply after a large trade, so checking recent activity first is worthwhile.

Fees and royalties

A marketplace fee and a creator royalty come out of the bid before it reaches your wallet, plus network gas to settle. Some bid-based venues make royalty optional, which raises what you net from the same nominal bid, so compare the net proceeds rather than the headline offer.

Instant sale vs listing, and staying safe

A bid sits below the ask because the bidder takes on the risk of holding the token until a future buyer appears. Listing can net more if someone matches it, but there is no certainty. Instant selling removes that uncertainty by locking in the deepest current bid.

Read every signature request, reject any approval broader than the single token and amount being sold, and never enter a seed phrase anywhere — no marketplace or bidder ever needs it.
