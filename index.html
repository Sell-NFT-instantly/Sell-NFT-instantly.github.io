How do I Sell NFT Instantly?

Connect your wallet, select an NFT and check for an eligible buyer offer. When an executable offer is available, review the net payout and approve the sale in your wallet. Live offers are not currently available on this site.

How is an instant NFT offer calculated?

Collection floor, recent sales, traits, rarity and buyer demand help inform a valuation. An instant offer also depends on available liquidity. A displayed estimate is not a guaranteed sale price.

Can every NFT be sold instantly?

No. An instant sale needs a valid buyer offer for that NFT or its collection, on a supported network. Rare traits alone do not guarantee demand or an offer.

What crypto do I receive, and are there fees?

The executable offer determines the payout currency and network. Review service fees, any royalties and network costs before signing. The example in this app uses ETH and illustrative fees; it cannot be accepted.

Does connecting my wallet sell or transfer my NFT?

No. Connecting shares your public account address. This app can read ERC-721 ownership on Ethereum, Base and Arbitrum. A sale would require a separate, explicit transaction approval; this site currently does not submit sale transactions.
